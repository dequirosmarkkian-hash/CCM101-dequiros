# Reflection – Laboratory 03: Multi-Cloud Explorer

Laboratory 03: Multi-Cloud Explorer helped me understand the differences between major cloud computing platforms such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Through this activity, I learned that although these providers offer similar cloud services, they have different tools, features, pricing models, infrastructure, and areas of specialization.

One of the most important things I learned was how to research and compare cloud services based on their actual functions. For example, cloud providers offer services for computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and other technologies. Understanding the equivalent services between providers helped me see how organizations can choose a cloud platform depending on their specific requirements rather than simply choosing a provider based on popularity.

The activity also improved my research and documentation skills. I had to gather information about different cloud platforms, organize the information, and present the results in a clear way. I learned that reliable documentation is important when researching cloud technologies because cloud services and features can change over time.

Another lesson I gained from this laboratory is the importance of multi-cloud computing. Organizations may use more than one cloud provider to meet different business or technical requirements. A multi-cloud approach can provide flexibility and allow organizations to use specific services from different providers. However, it can also introduce challenges such as managing multiple platforms, learning different tools, monitoring resources, and maintaining security across environments.

Overall, Laboratory 03 gave me a better understanding of how cloud providers differ and how their services can be compared. It also helped me develop practical skills in cloud research, service comparison, documentation, and technical analysis. This activity increased my interest in cloud computing and gave me a stronger foundation for learning more advanced cloud technologies in the succeeding laboratories.
